Terms

Last updated: 19 September 2026

These cover using Hark while it is in beta. They are written to be read, not to be impressive. If something here is unclear, ask and we will rewrite it.

What you are getting

A macOS app that reads text aloud, and describes code instead of spelling it out. It is free during beta. If it stops being free, you will be told before that happens, not after.

What beta means

It will break sometimes. It depends on the Accessibility features of other apps, and when those apps change their interface, the part that finds the newest reply can stop working until we catch up. Reads can fail, and descriptions of code can be wrong.

Do not rely on it for anything where being misread matters. It is a convenience, not a source of truth.

Your part

Use it for text you are allowed to read. Do not use it to break the terms of the apps it reads from, and do not use it to process other people's material you have no right to.

The app needs Accessibility and Input Monitoring permission to work. Granting those is your decision and you can take them back whenever you want.

Code descriptions

Code blocks are sent to Google's Vertex AI, and Google's terms apply to that processing. Descriptions are generated by a model, so they can be incomplete or plainly wrong. Read the code if it matters.

You can switch this off in the app.

What we do not promise

The app is provided as it is. We do not promise it will always work, always be available, or always be free of faults. To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for loss that follows from using it, including anything you miss because a read failed or described something incorrectly.

Nothing here limits rights you have that cannot be limited.

Changes

We will update these as the app changes, and the date at the top will tell you when. Carrying on using the app after a change means the new version applies.

Ending it

Stop using it and delete ~/.config/hark/. That is the whole process. We can also stop providing the app, and during beta that could happen without much notice.
